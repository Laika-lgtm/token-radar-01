How the value is built

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of the trading protocols in the same category, computed from this snapshot. A category with fewer than 3 trading comparables uses the snapshot-wide median. Clamped to 0.05–1.50.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) × 0.5.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.30. Age is the days from the protocol’s listing date to the snapshot date; 365 days when the snapshot records no date.
  • Chains — 1 + min(chains − 1, 4) × 0.03.

Scope: the board carries all 40 protocols on the snapshot’s watchlist, plus the rankable pre-token protocols below the $50M floor. Above the floor it carries the watchlist only — the snapshot holds no parent-protocol records, so a protocol the house left out above the floor may be a product of another protocol, and this radar does not rank what it cannot tell apart from a product. Wrapped, staked and bridged products, exchanges, versioned pools and exchange-branded products are never ranked.
